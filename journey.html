01

What got me into coding

2020 – 2021

AP Computer Science, senior year

High school · graduated 2021
Coding

I loved that in coding, if the logic is right, things behave exactly as you predict. Games were my favorite way to learn: the feedback is instant, and you pick up a lot of geometry and physics. For my final project I built my own Doodle Jump with Expo, and watching friends play it got me hooked.

02

The kitchen

After graduating I wasn't sure college was the right next step yet, so I got a job in a kitchen. It shaped me more than I expected.

2021 – 2023

Champion's Curry

Irvine, CA · eventually assistant kitchen manager
Work

I started as a dishwasher at a small food-court curry shop and worked my way up to cooking. With a team that small, everyone does everything: prep, cooking, service, and dishes.

What stuck: you get better by showing up and doing the small things a little better each day.
Around then

I almost went to culinary school

A fork in the road

I loved the kitchen enough to pay the deposit for culinary school, then decided not to go. I still wonder how things would have turned out if I had.

03

Bootcamp

Coding never really left my head, so I tried the fastest way back in.

While still cooking

UCI Coding Bootcamp, full-stack web development

Irvine, CA · 6 months
CodingStill on the line

While still working the line, I learned the MERN stack (MongoDB, Express, React, Node) and built my first deployed projects with a team. Along the way I learned agile sprints, Figma, GitHub, and how to debug without panicking.

After the bootcamp

The job search that didn't work out

I graduated into one of the toughest years for junior developers and didn't land anything. So I went back to school full time to build a real foundation in computer science.

What stuck: a "no" isn't the end. Sometimes it means you need to build a stronger foundation first.
04

All in at Irvine Valley College

Balancing school with work was tough, but these were some of the busiest and best years I've had.

2022 – 2025

Computer science at Irvine Valley College

A.S. Computer Science · May 2025
School

This time I learned the fundamentals the bootcamp skipped: object-oriented programming, data structures, discrete math, calculus, and linear algebra.

2023 – 2025

Wok chef at Din Tai Fung

Costa Mesa, CA
Work

A much bigger, busier kitchen, in a good way. Almost everyone in the back of house spoke Spanish, so I picked up a lot of phrases (some I won't repeat here) and made friends from all kinds of backgrounds.

2023 – 2024

Private math tutor

Irvine, CA
Teaching

By pure coincidence, I started tutoring algebra, geometry, and calculus for kids I met through community college. Turns out I love teaching, especially the moment a student stops being scared of a problem.

2023 – 2024

Project director, IVC Computer Science Club

Irvine Valley College
CodingTeaching

I ran 10+ workshops on React, Git, and web development and helped students deploy their first real projects. I also learned to plan events, make flyers people actually read, and found my hackathon crew.

What stuck: explaining something is the best way to find out whether you really understand it.

Stressful and overwhelming, but a lot of fun. My favorite parts were the mini games, the midnight snacks, and the friends I made.
